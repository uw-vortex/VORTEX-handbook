# Arterial stiffness reading essentials
The following are suggested starting points to review material related to arterial stiffness and early vascular aging. This is a very large field and your sub-specialization can branch quite far from this initial list.


* Townsend et al. 2015. Recommendations for improving and standardizing vascular research on arterial stiffness: A scientific statement from the American Heart Association. Hypertension 66:698-722.
* Cecelja & Chowienczyk 2013. Arterial stiffening: Causes and consequences. Artery Research 7: 22-27.
* Reference values for Arterial Stiffness' Collaboration 2010. Determinants of pulse wave velocity in healthy people and in the presence of cardiovascular risk factors: 'establishing normal and reference values'. Eur Heart J 31(19):2338-50.
* Mitchell. 2014. Arterial stiffness and hypertension: Chicken or Egg? Hypertension 64(2):210-214.
* Laurent et al. 2019. Concept of extremes in vascular aging: From early vascular aging to supernormal vascular aging. Hypertension 74(2):218-228.
* Nardone et al. 2018. Evidence for pressure-independent sympathetic modulation of central pulse wave velocity. J Am Heart Assoc 7(3):e007971.
* Stewart et al. 2006. Acute reduction of blood pressure by nitroglycerin does not normalize large artery stiffness in essential hypertension. Hypertension 48(3):404-410.
